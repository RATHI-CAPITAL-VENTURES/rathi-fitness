# Retro — v0.16.0, a pair of dumbbells (2026-10-08)

**Scope:** v0.16.0. The owner's report: "Hammer curl only adds weight assuming
total 25 lbs, not 25 on each side." A per-exercise dumbbell count, tonnage that
counts both, "each" on every display of a pair's weight, snapshot schema 8 with
`gym` still reading 7.

## What went well

- **One converter made the arithmetic a one-line change.** `SetEntry.tally`
  exists because nine call sites once built `Tally.Set` by hand and forgot
  `assisted`. Adding `implements` to the value and reading it there reached
  Today, past days, Trends, the activity grid, the journey and every snapshot
  total at once. The two places that did not go through it were exactly the two
  places that were wrong (below).
- **Nothing persisted a computed volume.** Every total is `Tally` over the sets
  at draw time, so there is no migration of history: the first launch re-tallies
  it. Checked, not assumed — `grep` for stored `volume` on the models finds only
  `AudioHub.volume`, which is loudness.
- **The schema bump did not have to blind the Mac.** Accepting 7 alongside 8
  costs one tuple and a footnote, and turns "RIA cannot read the gym log until
  the phone updates" into "RIA's tonnage is short, and says so".

## What was hard to understand

- **What "each" should do to records and trends.** Doubling would make the
  numbers "true" totals, and would also make a 25 lb pair outrank a 45 lb
  single, and suggest weights no rack has. The resolution — tonnage multiplies,
  everything that compares stays per dumbbell — is now written in
  `Tally.Set.implements` and DECISIONS, because the next person will ask again.
- **Whether a bump was owed.** `SNAPSHOT.md` says bump on a changed meaning, not
  on an addition; `volume`'s definition reads the same. The precedent settled
  it: schemas 2 and 4 were both bumps for `volume` changing on the same history.
- **The simulator test run hung** for twelve minutes on "PruneExplicitPrecompiled
  Modules" with a simulator CoreSimulator then reported as already shut down.
  Killing it and pinning `OS=26.2` as the Makefile does ran clean.
